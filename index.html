Peak Stream 7 is a short-form video channel that publishes highlights from live streams as YouTube Shorts, TikTok videos and Instagram Reels. Every clip credits the original creator and links to their channel.

To publish, we use a private tool that runs on our own computer. It edits clips into vertical format, adds captions, uploads them to our own Peak Stream 7 accounts through the official YouTube, TikTok and Instagram APIs, and reads view statistics of our own videos so we can learn which clips work best.

The tool is not offered to the public and is not used to access anyone else's account.
