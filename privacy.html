This policy describes how the Peak Stream 7 publishing tool ("the app") handles data. The app is operated by the owner of the Peak Stream 7 channels and is used only to manage those channels.

What the app accesses

  • Google / YouTube: permission to upload videos to the Peak Stream 7 YouTube channel and to read that channel's video statistics and YouTube Analytics (views, likes, comments, average view duration).
  • TikTok: permission to publish videos to the Peak Stream 7 TikTok account and to read basic profile information and that account's video statistics.
  • Instagram: permission to publish Reels to the Peak Stream 7 Instagram account and to read insights for that account's posts.

How data is used

Access tokens and statistics are used solely to publish our own videos and to understand which of our videos perform well. We do not access, collect or process data of any other users, and we do not sell, rent or share any data with third parties.

Where data is stored

Access tokens and statistics are stored only locally on the owner's computer, in files readable only by the owner. Nothing is stored on any server operated by us.

Third-party services

The app uses YouTube API Services. By using it you are also bound by the YouTube Terms of Service and the Google Privacy Policy. The app also uses the TikTok Content Posting API and the Instagram API, subject to the TikTok Privacy Policy and the Instagram Privacy Policy.

Retention and deletion

Statistics are kept for as long as the channels are active. Access can be revoked at any time: Google at myaccount.google.com/permissions, TikTok and Instagram in their app settings under connected apps. Revoking access stops all use; the local token files are then deleted. To request deletion of any data, email yoloclipzzz@gmail.com.
